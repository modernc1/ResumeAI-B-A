  • Cryptographically Hashed Identifiers: One-way cryptographically hashed (SHA-256) email addresses and internal system user IDs passed to verified attribution partners (Meta, TikTok) solely for non-monetary conversion attribution, strictly gated behind user tracking authorization.

3. Storage Architecture & No Public Storage Buckets

Unlike conventional web applications that drop customer files into publicly readable AWS S3 or Google Cloud Storage buckets, ResumeAI enforces a zero-trust, private relational database storage model:

🔒 Architectural Storage Policy
  • Zero Public Storage Buckets: The application does NOT upload, store, or expose raw user resumes or generated PDF/DOCX assets in publicly accessible cloud storage buckets or unauthenticated endpoints.
  • PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS): Resumes, extracted text, profile data, and cover letters are stored as structured binary/text rows directly inside private PostgreSQL database tables protected by database-enforced Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Cryptographic Isolation: Every database query requires a cryptographically validated JSON Web Token (JWT) signed by Supabase Auth. Even in the event of an API endpoint misconfiguration, database rules reject any operation where the requester's authenticated UUID does not match the record owner's user_id.
  • Encryption at Rest & in Transit: All data transmitted between the mobile client, backend infrastructure, and external APIs is encrypted via TLS 1.3 in transit and encrypted with AES-256 at rest.

4. Instant & Complete Account Deletion Procedure

ResumeAI provides our users with full, autonomous control over their personal data. We provide an immediate, self-service account deletion mechanism that operates in real time without requiring manual administrative approval.

4.1 How to Delete Your Account In-App

  1. Open the ResumeAI application on your mobile device.
  2. Tap the menu button to open the side Drawer or navigate to Settings.
  3. Scroll down and select "Delete Account".
  4. Review the permanent deletion warning and confirm your request.

Alternatively, users who have uninstalled the app or lost device access can initiate deletion by visiting our dedicated Web Account Deletion Portal or emailing Modernc12@gmail.com.

⚠️ Immediate Backend Purge Guarantee

Executing an account deletion triggers an automated, cascading backend wipe that immediately and permanently deletes records across all production tables:

  • Authentication & Profiles: User profile and authentication credentials purged from auth.users and public.profiles.
  • Resumes: All created, parsed, uploaded, or generated resumes purged from public.resumes and public.generated_resumes.
  • Cover Letters: All tailored letters purged from public.cover_letters.
  • AI Chat History: Complete multi-turn conversations purged from public.chat_sessions and public.chat_messages.
  • Ancillary Records: Unlocked premium templates, RevenueCat webhook audit logs, and client telemetry records tied to your UUID.

Note: Because this deletion purge is immediate and cascading, it cannot be undone or recovered.

6. Regulatory Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

We comply with international data protection frameworks, including the European Union / UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California privacy legislation (CCPA / CPRA):

6.1 Rights Under the GDPR (EEA & UK Users)

  • Right of Access: Request confirmation of whether your data is being processed and obtain a copy of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate or incomplete resume details directly in the app or upon request.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Demand total destruction of your data, executable instantly via the in-app "Delete Account" button.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: Restrict certain types of processing where legitimate grounds are contested.
  • Right to Data Portability: Export your resume data in standard portable formats (PDF, JSON, or text).
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke ATT tracking or marketing consent anytime via your device OS settings.

6.2 California Residents Notice (CCPA / CPRA)

  • Notice of Collection: We collect personal identifiers, commercial transaction info (subscriptions), internet activity (telemetry), and professional employment history.
  • Right to Know & Delete: California consumers may request disclosure of data categories collected and request complete deletion twice per 12-month period.
  • No Sale of Personal Information: We do NOT sell, rent, or lease your personal data or resume details to data brokers or third parties for monetary consideration.
  • Right to Opt-Out of Cross-Context Behavioral Advertising: You may limit cross-context behavioral advertising at any time by declining the Apple ATT prompt or disabling ad personalization in your Android OS settings.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge different prices, or provide a lesser quality of service to users who exercise their statutory privacy rights.
